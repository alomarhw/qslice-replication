Bugs4Q bugs ported as circuits, with each instruction mapped to the source line that creates it (data, read only).

circuits/<bug>.buggy.qasm|qpy  the circuit the buggy program builds, ported by hand from the buggy programs of Bugs4Q
                               (commit 7a71983), which are never executed; prepared for slicing when loaded
                               (experiments/icpc/b_reference.py, prepare)
instructions.csv               for each instruction of a circuit, its position and the line of the original buggy
                               program that creates it (empty when no line does)
bugs.csv                       the original file of each bug, its instruction count and how many are not mapped
units.csv                      the criteria: a bug, a criterion qubit, and the positions of the faulty instructions
                               (fault_site, ";"-separated); "primary" units are those whose fault is on the qubit
