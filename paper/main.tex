  \caption{Control and quantum dependence in a variational (VQE) ansatz, criterion $q_0$. After the loops,
  an auxiliary qubit is measured mid-circuit and conditions a flip of $q_0$. Each node is an operation as
  it executes, with the source line and loop iteration that created it, and both graphs place every node at
  the same position. (b)~The classical PDG reaches the auxiliary through the green, dashed control edge.
  Def-use ignores classical bits, so its graph is (b) without that edge, and its slice also loses the
  shaded, outlined operations. The classical PDG treats the control of a CNOT as read only. It lacks the
  orange, dashed edges, so its shaded slice of $q_0$ misses the outlined dependences. (c)~The QDG has these edges as quantum edges, and its slice is exactly the dependences
  of $q_0$. The QPDG would also add the purple operations, which are not dependences. Graphs,
  slices and fidelities at $\ket{0\dots0}$ are computed by the study's code from the program shown.}
  \label{fig:ansatz}
\end{figure*}
