\section{Implications for Comprehension Tools}\label{sec:implications}

The results turn into advice for those who build slicers and other comprehension aids for quantum programs, for the
developers who read their output, and for research on both.

\paragraph*{What a slicer must do}
A slicer must let every gate write all its qubits. Reading the control of a gate as unchanged is the one choice
behind \numCpdgMissControlPct{} of the dependences the classical PDG misses in RQ1, and it is the reading that the QCPG states and that an order table invites
when it marks the control apart from the target. A representation built for pattern detection can keep that
distinction for reporting, but a slicer that reads it as a dependence graph will hide code that can change the
qubit. A slicer must also follow measured bits to the operations they condition, since without them def-use is wrong
on \numDuDynamicUnsound{} of the \numNDynamicPairs{} pairs in dynamic circuits. It should not add entanglement
edges, which add only code that cannot change the criterion, \numYzLcExcessRatio{} times as much as the QDG keeps
beyond the dependences, and turn a pass of milliseconds into a graph that takes \numCostBuildDepthOneSixty{}~s to
build at the largest depth of RQ4. Because a single loop iteration can put a line in a slice, a slicer should show the iterations that put each
line there, so that the developer does not read a whole loop body as relevant.

\paragraph*{What a developer should not trust}
A slice cannot be validated by its outcomes. Of the wrong classical slices, \numFdCpdgZMatchPct{} reproduce the
outcome histogram of the whole program, and the Bugs4Q example of Section~\ref{sec:example} shows that a fault can
leave every single-qubit histogram unchanged. A check that compares histograms accepts exactly the slices that are
wrong in phase or in correlation, so a tool that offers such a check should compare the criterion's reduced state, or
at least its joint outcomes in more than one basis. Pruned slices answer a narrower question than the one a developer
asks. A slice pruned by commutation is correct for the observable at the circuit's angles, and Qiskit's pass is the
right tool to estimate that observable, but it drops dependences, recalling \numLcqRecall{} of them, and it fails
on \numRobLcqParamFail{} of \numRobLcqParamN{} variational pairs when the angles change. A state-based slice is correct for its one input, stays correct for random inputs on only
\numHThreeRate{} of the pairs, contains the fault \numHFourDiffAbs{} points less often than the QDG slice, and can
remove the noisy gates a developer is trying to diagnose. A tool that offers either should label it as a view for one
observable or one input and keep the QDG slice as the default.

\paragraph*{What a slice cannot show}
The QDG slice contained the fault in \numRqThreeContainLc{} of the units, and every unit it missed is an omission
fault, a removed control, gate or preparation, or a gate moved to another qubit. No slice can contain an operation
that the program lacks, nor follow an interaction that the fault has removed. When the slice of a qubit leaves out a qubit the developer expected it to depend on, that absence is itself
the clue. A tool can make it visible by listing the qubits each slice contains and, by the duality of
Proposition~\ref{prop:dual}, the qubits each operation reaches, at no extra cost.

\paragraph*{What remains open}
Slices narrowed where to look but did not improve mutation-based fault ranking, and given a slice, no language model
localized faults better than with the whole circuit by more than a fraction of a point. The models did miss
dependences that a slicer finds by rule, and Sonnet recalled \numTOneSonnetKick{} and the larger Qwen model
\numTOneQwenMidKick{} of those through kickback, so a slice is better used to give a model or a developer the dependences
than to rank suspects. Whether slices shorten the manual reading that developers report does not
scale~\cite{zappin2026challenges} is a question for a study with developers, which the semantics and the measured
rules of this paper make possible to design.
