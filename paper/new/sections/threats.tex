\section{Threats to Validity}\label{sec:threats}

\paragraph*{Construct validity}
The dependence set is measured with \numInfluencePerturbations{} random perturbations per operation at
\numInfluenceInputs{} inputs, so it can miss true dependences but never adds false ones, and the QDG's distance to it
may be smaller than reported. A check with \numGcInputs{} inputs, \numGcEntangledInputs{} of them entangled, and
\numGcPerturbations{} perturbations found only \numGcDepsNew{} new dependences, \numGcDepsNewPct{} of
\numGcDepsBefore{}, and left the QDG's recall unchanged. The dependence set and the wrong slices were checked at $\ket{0\dots0}$,
which RQ2 extends to random inputs. Each criterion is a single qubit, and Proposition~\ref{prop:joint} extends the QDG to
sets but not the pruning rules. Fault containment measures where a developer would look, not whether a developer finds
the fault. A unit is a fault that changes a qubit's measured distribution, so faults visible only in phase are left
out, which if anything favors the classical rules. The QPDG is reimplemented from prose, with every reading logged before any run and three variants
bracketing the plausible ones. Qiskit's pass applies to \numLcqApplicable{} of the \numNPairs{} pairs, since it refuses
circuits with measurements and some of its outputs cannot be matched back.

\paragraph*{Internal validity}
Errors in the slicers or the reference simulator are guarded against by the agreement with Qiskit's simulation, the
\numCatEntries{} hand-derived cases, and the nesting of the rules on every pair, which would expose a slicer that
drops or adds operations. The soundness proof is mechanized for an abstract model of local
operations, in which a measurement and the gate its bit conditions form one operation, and that quantum operations
satisfy the model is argued on paper. H1 and H2 were known in advance and hold by
construction, and every result outside the five hypotheses is exploratory. The Claude models accept only their default sampling, so their
answers can differ between calls, and the models may have seen the public benchmark circuits in training.

\paragraph*{External validity}
The circuits with an exact ground truth have at most \numSizeAMaxQubits{} qubits in corpus~A and \numSubjLMaxQubits{} in corpus~L, and
\numNDynamicPairs{} pairs come from dynamic circuits. The proofs hold for every circuit, but the measured distances
between the rules may change with width and feedback. Benchmark circuits are not programs written by developers, the
\numBugsFourQUnits{} Bugs4Q units are the only real faults, and seeded faults may not match real ones in their mix. The
gate check uses one angle per gate, the noise analysis depolarizing noise only, and loops that run on the device are
not studied.
