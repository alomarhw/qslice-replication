\section{Conclusion}\label{sec:b-conclusion}

Quantum programs can be sliced statically, on the source developers write. A quantum layer over srcML and srcSlice
reads the quantum role of each call, reconstructs the circuit a program builds, and slices every qubit along the gates
and measured bits that can affect it, loops on the device included, showing the result as lines, iterations and the
classical statements behind them. On thousands of real programs it reconstructs the circuits Qiskit builds, slices them
as their light cone does, takes a fraction of a second per program, and asks a developer to read about a quarter of a
program. Widening the role table, linking values returned from functions, and a study of how developers use the slices
are the next steps.
